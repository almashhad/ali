## PHASE 1 — RESEARCH DISTILLATION (what world-class brands actually do)

**Spacing as luxury.** Across Creed Regent Street, Amouage Mall of Oman, Aesop's 300+ stores, and Diptyque Maison, the dominant signal of luxury is *breathing room* — fewer products, more empty surface. Cluttered = cheap, regardless of materials.

**Material vocabulary of high-end fragrance retail (2024–2026):**
- **Stone:** Verde Luana marble (Creed), cream travertine + raw greige sandstone (Amouage), Italian marble shelf-tops, Calacatta, alabaster, onyx.
- **Wood:** American walnut panelling (Creed), red cedar treated with *uzukuri* technique (Le Labo Tokyo), oak shelving (Diptyque), mahogany Queen Anne table (Nonfiction NYC).
- **Metal:** Brushed brass (Creed, Initio), polished black stainless steel + gold leaf (Amouage), dark ironmongery (Jo Malone), bronze hardware.
- **Glass:** Museum-grade vitrines, illuminated 3D shards (Creed window), bespoke acrylic, frosted diffusers.
- **Soft elements:** Felt (Initio), wool carpet (Diptyque), velvet trays, linen, silk.

**Lighting principles repeated everywhere:**
1. High-CRI LED (Ra90+) — never standard fluorescent.
2. Dimmable, focused — every product gets its own pool of light.
3. Color temperature 2700–3000K (warm, "candlelight") — never 4000K+.
4. Backlighting/under-shelf glow to make amber liquid *come alive*.
5. Glorifier platforms: clear risers + mirrored panels + diffused halo = +25% perceived value.

**Information card hierarchy (what works):**
- Material: heavy stock (300gsm+), uncoated, cotton or hot-foil stamped.
- Typography: 2 weights max, generous letterspacing, never crowded.
- Content order: Name → notes (top/heart/base) → poetic line → price (optional, discreet).
- Bilingual: Arabic right-aligned + English left-aligned, baseline aligned.
- Size: 8–10cm wide × 6–8cm tall — never bigger than the bottle.

**Spatial principles:**
- "Experience zones" (where customer pauses, smells, reads) separated from "product zones" (where they browse).
- Height variation: 3 levels min — eye level (~140cm seated, ~165cm standing), waist level, low display.
- One "hero" per display (Maison Diptyque's marble fireplace, Amouage's 3m gold sphere) — the rest supports.

## PHASE 2 — FIXED CONSTRAINTS (applied to every single design below)

| Constraint | Value |
|---|---|
| Table width | **60 cm** (narrow — critical) |
| Table length | **400 cm** |
| Max units on table | **6** |
| Min gap between units | **25 cm** |
| Max unit footprint depth | **20 cm** (40cm of table breathes clear) |
| Bottle dimensions | ~8cm W × 14cm tall, amber/gold rectangular |
| Required components per unit | Bottle + riser + info card |
| Card content (bilingual) | VIBE / name / top–heart–base notes / one poetic line |
| Store palette to harmonize with | Warm walnut walls, amber LED under-shelf, white display surfaces, Chesterfield sofa, ceiling spots |
| Forbidden | Clutter, cool 4000K+ light, anything competing with the wall shelves |

**Layout math (used in every ASCII diagram):**
With 6 units × 20cm = 120cm of unit footprint, the remaining 280cm is gap space → 280 ÷ 5 gaps = **56cm between unit centres** (≥25cm clear minimum). End margins ≈ 20cm each side. This is the rhythm: **20 → [56] → 20 → [56] → 20 → [56] → 20 → [56] → 20 → [56] → 20**.

## DESIGN 01 — The Minimalist Japanese
**Design Philosophy:** Negative space is the actual product; the bottle is a quiet guest on a silent surface. Subtract until subtraction itself stings.
**Inspiration Sources:** Aesop Shinjuku (chipped plaster vs. stainless), Le Labo Kyoto Machiya (uzukuri cedar), Muji House aesthetic.

### Table Layout (400cm × 60cm)
```
[20]┌──┐[56]┌──┐[56]┌──┐[56]┌──┐[56]┌──┐[56]┌──┐[20]
    │01│    │02│    │03│    │04│    │05│    │06│
    └──┘    └──┘    └──┘    └──┘    └──┘    └──┘
```
Unit spacing: 56cm centre-to-centre, 36cm clear. Units: 6.

### Display Stand (Riser)
- Material: solid bleached Hinoki cypress block.
- Dimensions: 12cm W × 12cm D × 4cm H (low, square, anchoring).
- Finish: hand-planed, no varnish; sealed only with diluted shellac.
- Color: warm bone (Munsell 2.5Y 8.5/2).
- Cost/unit: AED 180.

### Info Card / Plaque
- Material: 350gsm Awagami Kozo washi, hand-deckled edge.
- Dimensions: 8cm W × 6cm H.
- Position: flat on table, 4cm in front of the bottle.
- Angle: flat (0°).
- Typography: Yu Mincho for Japanese sensibility / IBM Plex Sans Arabic / Caslon — 8pt body, 14pt name, 1.5x leading.
- Content layout:
  ```
  VIBE
  ─────────────
  Oud Suhayl
  عود السهيل
  Top: bergamot, pink pepper
  Heart: rose absolute
  Base: oud, ambergris
  "كما يطلع نجم السهيل من جنوب الصحراء"
  ```
- Color scheme: paper white / ink black / single vermillion stamp lower-right.

### Lighting Recommendation
- Type: ceiling-mounted MR16 pin-spot per unit (6 fixtures, ~5° beam).
- Color temperature: 2700K, CRI 95+.
- Position: 220cm ceiling, aimed at 25° onto bottle centre.
- Effect: each bottle is an island of light on a dark sea of table.

### Materials Shopping List
- Hinoki block — Tanaka Lumber via UAE Japan-imports, AED 1,080 total.
- Washi paper — Falconcity Stationery Dubai, AED 220.
- MR16 spots — iGuzzini Le Perroquet, Lightland Trading Dubai, AED 4,200.
- Hand-stamps — Sharjah Calligraphy Souk, AED 350.

### Why This Works for Vibe
The walnut walls already carry tonal weight; a near-empty central table reads as confident restraint and lets the wall product breathe. 36cm clear between units is wider than most boutiques dare — that is exactly why it photographs as luxury.

### Potential Weakness
Risks looking *too* austere for a Levantine clientele that often associates richness with abundance. Mitigate by ensuring the wall shelves remain visually full.

## DESIGN 02 — The Arabic Heritage Master
**Design Philosophy:** The mashrabiya is not nostalgia — it is the most sophisticated screen ever designed for filtered light on precious objects. Use its geometry as the *bones* of every riser.
**Inspiration Sources:** Amouage Mall of Oman (greige sandstone + gold sphere), Tariq Al-Jaidah's mashrabiya reinterpretations, the wooden lattices of Old Manbij citadel courtyards.

### Table Layout (400cm × 60cm)
```
[20]╔══╗[56]╔══╗[56]╔══╗[56]╔══╗[56]╔══╗[56]╔══╗[20]
    ║▦▦║    ║▦▦║    ║▦▦║    ║▦▦║    ║▦▦║    ║▦▦║
    ╚══╝    ╚══╝    ╚══╝    ╚══╝    ╚══╝    ╚══╝
```
Six brass mashrabiya cubes; the bottle sits inside a small inset window. Units: 6.

### Display Stand (Riser)
- Material: laser-cut 2mm brass sheet, antiqued; cube 14×14×14cm.
- Pattern: 8-point star (najmah thumaniyah) with 18mm voids.
- Finish: warm brushed brass, hand-darkened in the recesses.
- Color: aged brass (RAL 1036).
- Cost/unit: AED 620 (laser-cut, folded, welded, antiqued).

### Info Card / Plaque
- Material: laser-engraved 2mm brass plate, mounted on raw greige sandstone slab.
- Dimensions: 9cm W × 7cm H plate on a 12×9cm stone base.
- Position: beside the cube, to the right of the bottle from customer view.
- Angle: 15° back-tilt for legibility.
- Typography: Thuluth or Diwani for the perfume name in Arabic; Trajan Pro for the Latin. Notes in Frutiger Arabic 8pt.
- Content layout:
  ```
        VIBE
   ▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒▒
   اَلْمَجْلِس
   Al-Majlis
   عود · ورد طائفي · مسك
   Oud · Taif Rose · Musk
   "حيث يُصبّ القهوة، يولد الكلام."
  ```
- Color scheme: brass on warm sand — entirely earth-toned.

### Lighting Recommendation
- Type: hidden LED puck *inside* the brass cube (3W, 2700K) to throw the mashrabiya pattern outward across the table.
- Color temperature: 2700K, CRI 95.
- Position: bottom-inside of cube, aimed up at the bottle base; pattern projects onto walnut walls behind.
- Effect: at night the table itself becomes a lantern — geometric shadows dance across the walnut. This is the *single most photographable feature in the store*.

### Materials Shopping List
- Brass sheet 2mm — Al Quoz metal market (Hamdan Metals), AED 1,800 raw, AED 3,720 laser-cut.
- Greige sandstone slabs — Quarry Stone Trading Sharjah, AED 480.
- Brass plates engraved — Sharjah Engraving Souk, AED 720.
- LED pucks — Lightland, AED 660.

### Why This Works for Vibe
Manbij sits in the Levantine cultural corridor; mashrabiya is *home language*. The pattern reading across the walnut at night transforms the boutique into an experience — customers will photograph the shadows, not just the bottles. This is free marketing.

### Potential Weakness
Brass requires re-polishing every 4–6 months in humid air or it will green. Schedule maintenance.

### Image Generation Prompt
> Luxury Arabic heritage perfume display, six brass mashrabiya cube risers with 8-point star pattern, amber perfume bottles inside cubes, warm 2700K LED projecting geometric shadows onto walnut wall behind, raw greige sandstone info bases with engraved brass plaques, narrow 4m walnut shelf, candlelight mood, Amouage Mall of Oman aesthetic, shot on Hasselblad, 85mm, f5.6, AgX color management, --ar 21:9 --style raw

### Info Card / Plaque
- Material: museum-card cotton stock, 400gsm.

- Angle: 5° tilt via small clip.
- Typography: Bodoni 8pt (free font).

- Position: ceiling.

**Total riser cost: AED 660 for all 6** vs. AED ~7,000+ for premium designs. The lighting budget eats the savings, but the result still photographs as upper-mid-luxury.

Vinyl can lift at edges after 2 years. Plan vinyl refresh on 24-month cycle (~AED 200).

### THE HYBRID — "VIBE SIGNATURE TABLE"

**Combines the structural strengths of #27 + #23 + #30 + #49 + #50:**

> **Six solid walnut mihrab-arch pedestals** (from Design 23) with **damascene mother-of-pearl + brass-wire inlay across the arch frame** (from Design 49), wrapped at the *base* with a **cognac saddle-leather collar with hand-stitching** (from Design 30). Each pedestal has a **hidden 2400K LED inside the arch** that simultaneously silhouettes the bottle from within AND **casts an 8-point star perforation pattern through a brass mashrabiya panel onto the walnut wall behind** (from Design 27). The pedestals follow **Fibonacci dimensions** 13×8×21cm (from Design 48). The ones in the centre use solid material; the two outer pedestals use **MDF + brushed-brass vinyl wrap + real brass arch-edge band** as the budget extension (from Design 50) — so the design scales: four "hero" pedestals + two "supporting cast" pedestals = the customer doesn't see the difference, but the budget breathes.

#### Hybrid Specification Sheet

**Pedestal type A (four units, premium):**
- Solid American black walnut, mihrab arch carved 16cm H × 12cm W × 8cm deep, dimensioned 13cm W × 21cm H × 8cm D.
- Mother-of-pearl + brass-wire damascene inlay around the arch edge.
- Cognac saddle-leather collar around the base, saddle-stitched in tan thread.
- Hidden 2400K 5W LED inside the arch + reed-switch (optional reveal mode).

**Pedestal type B (two units, budget-extender):**
- MDF core wrapped in Cover Styl' walnut-grain architectural vinyl (a very good match for solid walnut from 50cm distance).
- Real solid brass arch-edge band (the only "real" material — this is what sells the illusion).
- Same internal LED + reed-switch.
- Same engraved brass disc — visually identical to type A.

**Behind-the-table mashrabiya screen (additional element):**
- Three full-height brushed brass mashrabiya panels (8-point star perforation) mounted on the walnut wall behind the table, 80cm W × 200cm H each, with hidden 2700K LED behind each.
- Casts the iconic geometric shadow pattern *over* the table and *onto* the wall — turning the whole zone into a single composition.

**Info cards (six identical):**
- Cream cotton 600gsm, 8.5cm × 5.3cm (φ proportion).
- Thuluth Arabic name + Trajan English + Mercury 7pt notes.
- Single hand-painted gold dot lower-right (handmade signature).

**Lighting plan:**
1. One 2700K MR16 pin-spot per bottle (6 fixtures, 8° beam, ceiling).
2. Six hidden 2400K LED pucks inside the arches.
3. Three hidden 2700K LED bars behind the mashrabiya panels.
4. One linear 3000K cove wash high above the table (ambient fill, 30% intensity).

This four-layer lighting plan is the secret weapon — it creates depth, shadow play, silhouette, and ambient warmth from one display.

#### Installation Instructions

1. **Power infrastructure:** before pedestals are placed, route 12V DC wiring through the table to a single concealed driver under the table. Each pedestal terminates in a single connector through the table-top (drill 6 × 8mm holes pre-marked in the 4m walnut surface).
2. **Mashrabiya panels:** mount on the wall *first* — 200cm tall, three panels evenly spaced behind the table footprint. Hidden warm LED strip mounts to the back of each panel; conceal the wiring inside a 30mm furring strip behind the panel.
3. **Pedestals:** stand them on the table at the 56cm-centre rhythm specified in the constraints. Wire each to its LED puck through the pre-drilled hole. Reed switches: glue magnet to underside of each bottle base so lifting the bottle triggers a small light pulse (optional, debate with the user before enabling).
4. **Spots:** the ceiling MR16s should already exist — re-aim each to dead-centre over its pedestal at 25° from vertical. Use a 8° optic insert. Dim to 70% (full intensity reads harsh).
5. **Cove wash:** mount the linear 3000K bar inside the existing under-shelf channel of the wall shelving — this is the *ambient backbone* that keeps the boutique warm regardless of pedestal lighting.
6. **Cards:** place after final cleaning. They are paper — handle with cotton gloves once gold dots are applied.

#### Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Damascene artisan delay | Order at W1; build Type B units in parallel as fallback hero (if Type A is late, swap visually identical pieces) |
| Walnut moisture cracking in Manbij climate | Specify kiln-dried 8% MC walnut; acclimate 7 days on-site before final install |
| Brass tarnish (greens in humid air) | Apply Renaissance Wax sealer on installation day; quarterly re-wax |
| LED puck failure | Buy 3 spare pucks; each replaceable from above without removing pedestal |
| Vinyl edge lift on Type B over 24 months | Plan AED 200 vinyl refresh at year-2 mark |
